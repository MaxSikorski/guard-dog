Guard Dog

A self-hosted watchdog for your Bitcoin and Nostr stack. It knows what you run, notices the moment something turns dangerous, and tells you exactly what to do.

Why I'm building this

Doing everything right wasn't enough.

In July 2026 the Coldcard hack drained about $116M from hardware wallets whose firmware had quietly made weak seeds since 2021. The people who got hit were careful self-custodians. A couple of months later, Alby Hub had a wallet-takeover flaw that hit people running old versions open to the internet, even though a fix had been out for about a year. If your money and identity live on your own infrastructure, you're your own security team. Guard Dog is the member of that team that never sleeps.

What you tell it
Your hardware wallets and apps, and any git repo you paste in, like someone's custom app you run.
How it works
Knows what you run
It finds every service and version on your node, without ever touching your keys.
Tells you when it turns dangerous
It matches what you run against security advisories, including "only if it's open to the internet", and sends step-by-step fixes.
CriticalHighMediumNo known issues
Sees what an attacker sees
Open ports, router mappings, Tor and tunnels, plus a probe from a buddy's Guard Dog. Scout, its own network discovery, spots forgotten nodes and attacks like ARP spoofing.
Watches for a break-in
Watch-only alerts for your wallets, npub and Lightning node, plus decoy tripwires that prove someone got in.

Cut internet access first: reply YES-7F3K to remove the router mapping. 2. Update Alby Hub to 1.24.0. 3. Change the Hub's unlock password. Checked 3 min ago · 5 blind spots · No known issues is not the same as secure.

Example. Alerts can also go out by ntfy, Signal, Telegram or email. AI is optional: it works with your own local models, and everything important works without it.

What it will never do
Hold your keys or move your funds
Send your setup's details anywhere you didn't choose
Change anything without your one-tap approval
Tell you you're "secure"Only "no known issues found", with when it checked and what it couldn't see.
Scan anybody else's machines
