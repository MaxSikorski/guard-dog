Guard Dog

A self-hosted watchdog for your Bitcoin and Nostr stack. It knows what you run, notices the moment something turns dangerous, and tells you exactly what to do.

Why I'm building this

Doing everything right isn't enough anymore.

AI has made finding holes in software cheap, fast and available to anyone. Swarms of AI agents can comb through open-source code around the clock, and the people attacking our stack have those tools too. 2026 made it clear:

Coldcard, JulyAbout $116M drained from careful self-custodians: weak seeds since 2021, and updating wasn't enough. The first shot across the bow.Funds lost
BTCPay Server, August and NovemberFirst, stolen access files drained merchants' Lightning nodes. Then hot wallets on outdated servers were drained, about $1.6M.Funds lost
Alby Hub, SeptemberA wallet-takeover flaw hit old versions open to the internet, though a fix had been out for about a year.Takeover
LNbits, NovemberA wave of attacks on servers older than 1.7.0. Operators had to rotate keys and credentials, not just update.Funds lost
And it keeps comingOne thing after another, faster than any of us can follow alone.Ongoing

If your money and identity live on your own infrastructure, you're your own security team. Guard Dog is the member of that team that never sleeps, putting the same kind of AI on your side.

What it watches
Bitcoin
Bitcoin Core and Knots, BTCPay Server, Fulcrum, electrs, mempool.
Lightning
LND, Core Lightning, Alby Hub and LDK nodes, plus the dashboards around them.
Ecash
Cashu mints and Fedimint guardians and gateways.
Nostr
strfry and other relays, Blossom servers, NIP-46 bunkers, NIP-05 and Wallet Connect.
Underneath
Umbrel, StartOS, RaspiBlitz, myNode, nix-bitcoin, Docker, Tor, SSH and your home network.
What you tell it
Your hardware wallets and apps, and any git repo you paste in, like someone's custom app you run.
How it works
Knows what you run
It finds every service and version on your node, without ever touching your keys.
Tells you when it turns dangerous
It matches what you run against security advisories, including "only if it's open to the internet", and sends step-by-step fixes.
CriticalHighMediumNo known issues
Sees what an attacker sees
Open ports, router mappings, Tor and tunnels, plus a probe from a buddy's Guard Dog. Scout, its own network discovery, spots forgotten nodes and attacks like ARP spoofing.
Watches for a break-in
Watch-only alerts for your wallets, npub and Lightning node, plus decoy tripwires that prove someone got in.
An alert, as it arrives by Nostr DM
GUARD DOG · CRITICAL Alby Hub is reachable from the internet and has a wallet-takeover flaw. You run 1.18.2 · fixed in 1.19.0 · safest: 1.24.0 What to do: 1. Cut internet access first: reply YES-7F3K to remove the router mapping. 2. Update Alby Hub to 1.24.0. 3. Change the Hub's unlock password. Checked 3 min ago · 5 blind spots · No known issues is not the same as secure.

Example. Alerts can also go out by ntfy, Signal, Telegram or email. AI is optional: it works with your own local models, and everything important works without it.

What it will never do
Hold your keys or move your funds
Send your setup's details anywhere you didn't choose
Change anything without your one-tap approval
Tell you you're "secure"Only "no known issues found", with when it checked and what it couldn't see.
Scan anybody else's machines
Help build it

I know I'm missing things.

If you run your own node, relay or mint, you already know something Guard Dog needs to know. Guard Dog is open source and pre-alpha, so now's the best time to shape it. Pick whatever fits you:

Test it on your setup
Umbrel, StartOS, RaspiBlitz, myNode, nix-bitcoin or plain Docker. Tell me what it found, what it missed and what confused you.
Teach it a service
Every service Guard Dog knows is a small data file. Add the one you run, or fix how it reads a version.
Curate advisories
Turn a project's security notice into a clear advisory with plain-English steps to fix it.
Break it, white hat
Try to attack Guard Dog itself: prompt injection, privileges, the alerts. Report it privately and get credit.
Write code
A Rust core and a vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript interface. No frameworks.
Design, docs and translations
Make it clearer for the next person who runs their own stack for the first time.
Read the contributing guide Open an issue
Found a vulnerability in Guard Dog?Please report it privately, never in a public issue.
